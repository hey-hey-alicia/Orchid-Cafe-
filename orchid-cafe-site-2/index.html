hoa lan · the orchid

Resilience, grace, and a family table.

In Vietnamese culture the orchid stands for resilience and quiet elegance — a flower that blooms wherever it's planted. That's our story too. Our family brought the recipes we grew up with into the brunch culture we now call home.

When we took over this St Leonards corner, we kept the name and the warmth that came with it. Same spot, same community — now with phở broth simmering from early morning, bánh mì on the grill, and phin coffee dripping slow so your lunch doesn't have to.
